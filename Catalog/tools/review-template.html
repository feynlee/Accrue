Where the content comes from

Cues are written for Accrue from strength-and-conditioning texts and studies, listed under each exercise. openGym's MIT-licensed names are kept as a cross-reference id. openGym's Gym visual pictures and clips are not used anywhere, and neither are wger or other share-alike images.

Why drawn, not collected

The open image sets mix photos, line art and 3D renders, and none of them show shoulder-blade position or arm rotation. One drawn figure keeps the style consistent and can light up exactly the joint a cue is about.

How it ships

Each exercise is a small JSON file: text plus keyframes. The app draws it natively in SwiftUI in the Lime night and Sage and lime themes, so the full catalog stays a few hundred kilobytes and works offline.
