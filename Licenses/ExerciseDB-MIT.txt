Exercise names and ids

Accrue's exercise list (AccrueKit/Sources/AccrueKit/Resources/catalog.json, "exercises") takes
its exercise names and ids from the openGym catalogue (github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym).
No openGym description, instruction text, translation, picture or clip is included.

About 1,320 of those names come from ExerciseDB v1 by AscendAPI, through
