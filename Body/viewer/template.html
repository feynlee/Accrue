Seated dumbbell shoulder press

A MakeHuman body (CC0) on our own shoulder rig, posed from a short JSON of key poses. The collarbone, shoulder blade and upper arm move together with a measured rhythm. Switch to X-ray to see the shoulder blades glide and turn.

How it is made. The body mesh, skeleton and skin weights are MakeHuman's (CC0). We add a shoulder-blade bone and drive the collarbone, shoulder blade and humerus from one number, the arm's elevation, using scapulohumeral rhythm from Ludewig et al. 2009. Each exercise is a few key poses in JSON; a Blender script bakes them into this GLB in about 7 seconds. The same file converts to USDZ for the iPhone app.

What to check. In X-ray from the back, the lower tip of each shoulder blade swings out about 10 cm as the arms go up, and the blade tilts back against the ribs. At the top the collarbones lift a little: that small shrug is correct overhead.

Not yet. Skin over the shoulder blades is a first pass, the muscle map is hand-placed, and the hands hold the dumbbells loosely. Faces and hands use MakeHuman defaults.
