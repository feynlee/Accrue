Choices to tweak

  • The level stepper replaces weight for bodyweight moves: Wall, Incline, Knees, Standard, Decline, each with its share of body weight.
  • Should the demo show the working muscles in red here, or stay plain gray during a set?
  • Phase and breath pills could also be spoken or played as a tone at each phase change.

  • Six steps, or fewer (merge Bottom into Lower)?
