Choices to tweak

  • Skin or Outline as the default look for cardio? Outline reads better at a glance; skin shows the body shape and the clothes.
  • Floors from the phone's barometer (16 steps a floor here), or only steps?
  • The pace stepper uses the Compendium of Physical Activities: slow 4.5, steady 6.8, fast 9.3 MET. Calories assume 70 kg until the profile has a weight.
